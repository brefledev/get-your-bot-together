# [Project name]

Ask the assistant to fill this from the current conversation and actual work, not send you a blank form. Keep it in chat or an approved private location. Replace the prompts below, omit irrelevant lines, and mark important gaps as “Unknown: …”. Ask only about gaps that affect the next step.

## Current brief

- **Goal:** What result is needed, for whom, and what will make it usable?
- **Assignment:** The owner's request and later corrections, with dates or accessible source references. Keep corrections that supersede older instructions clear.
- **Inputs and draft:** Actual source files or links and the current draft/version. Preserve original inputs; identify inaccessible sources instead of replacing them with guesses.
- **Scope:** What may change, what must stay, and where owner approval is required.
- **State:** What is complete, the evidence for it, and any material unknown or blocker. Separate planned checks from results actually observed.
- **Next:** One useful action, its owner, and where the result should return.
- **State owner:** One person or assistant responsible for integrating results and updating this brief.

After meaningful progress or a correction, the state owner updates the current draft, evidence and next action. Keep dated detail with the work rather than growing this brief into a transcript. If that owner cannot edit the saved brief, return the proposed update to the person who can.
