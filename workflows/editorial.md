# Return the finished article

Use this procedure for an article, proposal, memo or other written deliverable. Apply it in the current conversation, or give it to a worker with useful source access and editing tools.

## Complete editing procedure

> Identify the latest owner-supplied draft and applicable corrections. Preserve the owner's title, audience, substantive choices, voice and unaffected wording.
>
> Make a short acceptance list from the request. Read the actual draft and original evidence. Check new, changed and time-sensitive claims against current primary sources. Record documented features, observed results and recommendations accurately. Correct, narrow or remove a claim when its source fails to support it.
>
> Make the authorized revisions and follow the owner's citation style. Keep editorial notes alongside the reader's document. Use verified publication URLs. Compare related articles where their shared claims must agree.
>
> Check the completed artifact against the acceptance list and inspect rendered pages when delivering a formatted document. Return the complete revision and a short account of material changes and unresolved facts. Preserve the original. Publication follows the owner's applicable authorization.

## Tailor it to the work

Name the actual current draft, relevant corrections, source set, output format and destination. Save a reusable procedure when repeated work justifies it and the owner approves.

For a product article, useful acceptance criteria include current documentation, accurate personal/local/cloud distinctions, supported setup steps and readable source links. Choose the criteria that fit the assignment.

A separate worker needs the latest draft and original sources in its own accessible context. Use the [handoff](../templates/HANDOFF.md) and [finishing instruction](../setup/finish-work.md).

Mechanical checks can confirm that files open, local links resolve, required sections exist and pages render without clipping. Source relevance and voice require a reader's judgment. A claimed automated check can be exercised on an isolated copy.
