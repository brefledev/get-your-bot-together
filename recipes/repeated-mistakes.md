# Fix a repeated mistake

**Use this when:** you keep supplying the same correction.
**Leave with:** one adapted instruction, procedure step, or check.

## Start with evidence

Give your assistant a recent example and the correction you made. Ask it to compare the original request with the actual result.

> Find the specific step that led to this correction. Explain what the evidence shows and what remains uncertain. Suggest a small change that would catch this problem next time. Show the complete proposed change and try it on the example.

Separate a missing preference, an ambiguous brief, an unavailable source, and an omitted check. Each needs a different remedy.

## Check the improvement

Use a copy of the draft with one unsupported claim. Confirm that the assistant identifies that claim and gives the correct source locations for the supported ones. Preserve the original. Accept a clean result when the requirements are satisfied.

Save the procedure only after the owner chooses its location and scope. To undo it, restore the previous instruction or remove this exact addition. Evaluate another real task before making a broad reliability claim.
